===SHEET: 04_Mindfulness_Content===
R1|State | Duration | Practice | Purpose | Guided script
R2|Stressed | 3 | Breathing | Settle the body | Sit comfortably. Let your shoulders drop. Notice your breathing without changing it. Slowly breathe in. Slowly breathe out. Repeat for three minutes. When your mind wanders, gently return to the next breath.
R3|Restless | 5 | Grounding | Come back to the present | Notice 5 things you can see. Notice 4 things you can physically feel. Notice 3 sounds. Notice 2 smells or sensations. Notice 1 thing you are grateful to have in this moment. Finish with three slow breaths.
R4|Angry | 5 | Breathing | Create a pause before reacting | Do not try to force the anger away. Notice where it feels strongest in the body. Breathe in gently and make the exhale a little longer. Repeat. At the end, name one thing you can control in the next ten minutes.
R5|Distracted | 3 | Quiet focus | Return attention to one anchor | Choose one sound or the feeling of your feet on the floor. Stay with it for one breath at a time. When thoughts pull you away, notice that and return without judging yourself.
R6|Overwhelmed | 10 | Body scan | Slow down and notice | Start at your feet and move attention upward: feet, legs, abdomen, chest, shoulders, face. At each area, notice tension without needing to fix it. Breathe slowly and allow each area to soften if it naturally does.
R7|General calm | 5 | Quiet focus | Create a calm reset | Sit or lie comfortably. Notice the contact between your body and the surface supporting you. Follow five natural breaths. Notice sounds around you. Return to your breath. Finish by choosing one small, manageable next step.
